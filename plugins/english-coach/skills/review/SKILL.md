# English review

1. Read the journal files in `$ENGLISH_JOURNAL_DIR` (default `~/english-journal/`) (named `YYYY-MM-DD.md`) from the last 7 days. If I pass a number as an argument, use that many days instead.
2. Group the mistakes by grammar rule and rank them by how often they appear.
3. For the top 3 rules, give a deep explanation: the rule, why English works that way, common exceptions, and 3 examples taken from my own sentences (before → after).
4. List the vocabulary I keep overusing (e.g. "make", "do", "good", "help") with more precise alternatives and example sentences.
5. Point out one sentence-structure habit I should change (e.g. run-on sentences, missing subjects, word order) and show how to restructure it.
6. Write 10 exercises (fill-in-the-blank and rewrite-this-sentence) based on my real mistakes. Put the answers at the end, under a "Answers" heading.
7. Append a dated summary of the top recurring mistakes to `mistakes.md` in the journal folder so we can track progress over time. If earlier entries exist, compare and tell me which mistakes are improving and which are not.

Respond in English only. Keep explanations clear and simple, but go deep on the grammar.
